# Privacy policy

This policy covers the killer sudoku app published on Google Play under the package name
`se.undersphere.killersudoku`, built from this repository.

**The app does not collect, share or sell any personal data.**

- It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting service.
- It does not send your games, settings or statistics anywhere. Everything is stored only on
  your device, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.
- If the app crashes, it shows the error on screen. You can copy it or open a page to report it
  yourself; nothing is sent automatically.
- If you use Android's own backup, your device may include the app's data in that backup. That
  backup is controlled by you and your device's backup settings, not by the developer.
- The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this ever changes, for example if a future version adds an optional online feature, this
page will say what is collected and why before that version is released, and the feature will
be off unless you turn it on.
