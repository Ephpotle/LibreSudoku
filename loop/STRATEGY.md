## Stages (draft)

1. **What makes killers fail.** Research and our own measurements of which features
   drive difficulty; turn them into a hardness score and check it against puzzles of
   known difficulty.
2. **Extreme with a hardness scale.** A level above Challenge, each puzzle showing its
   hardness, generated fast enough to start at once.
3. **Distinction.** Records and badges for solving high-hardness puzzles, on the
   leaderboard he chooses.
4. **His app.** Name, home screen, upkeep.

## Current bottleneck (draft)

No one knows which features make a killer fail more players, and our rater score has
never been checked against real difficulty. A hardness scale built on guesses would
mislabel puzzles, so stage 1 comes first.

## Out of scope

Solving aids of any kind. Online features, accounts or crowd statistics (the app
stays offline). Classic sudoku polish beyond keeping it working.
