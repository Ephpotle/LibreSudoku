# Strategy

**Status: draft, not approved.** Waiting for Markus to confirm the north star and its
terms (asked 2026-11-22 in the Mainspring thread).

## North star (draft)

> "An ad-free killer sudoku app on my phone where I can always start a fair puzzle
> that I win less than 40% of the time."

Drafted 2026-11-22 from his words of 2026-10-25: "I try to always play a game that has
below 40% success rate for a challenge."

## Terms (proposed readings, not yet confirmed)

| Term | Reading | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Ad-free, my own app | The Ephpotle/LibreSudoku fork on Play internal testing, offline, no ads or tracking | First thread, 2026-10-25 |
| Below 40% success rate | His own clean win rate over the last 20 finished games at a level is under 40% | 2026-10-25, agreed to a personal win rate because the app is offline |
| Fair, "for a challenge" | One solution, reachable by logic; no feature does the thinking | 2026-11-08 rule |
| Always | A puzzle at that level starts at once, offline | Inferred; to confirm |

Open: whether a game finished after "Try again" counts as a win.

## Stages (draft)

1. **An honest measure.** The win rate counts only clean games, and the loop can see
   his numbers (screenshot or export).
2. **A level hard enough.** At least one level where his clean win rate is under 40%,
   and puzzles at that level start instantly.
3. **Calibrated.** The app points him at the level nearest 40% and keeps it there as
   he improves.
4. **His app.** Name, home screen and upkeep (backlog phase 3 and 4).

## Current bottleneck (draft)

The loop cannot see the one number the north star is about. His results live only on
his phone, the win rate still counts assisted and "Try again" games as wins, and no
one knows yet whether Challenge is already under 40% for him. Until that is measured,
work on harder puzzles is aimed blind.

## Out of scope

Solving aids of any kind. Online features, accounts or crowd statistics (the app
stays offline). Classic sudoku polish beyond keeping it working.
